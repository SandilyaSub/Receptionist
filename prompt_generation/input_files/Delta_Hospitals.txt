HEALTH CHECKUP PACKAGES:

Basic Health Checkup Package: ₹2000. Includes a complete blood haemogram, fasting blood sugar, total cholesterol, ECG, blood grouping, and a specialist consultation.
Master Health Checkup Package: ₹3000. Includes everything from the Basic package plus a lipid profile, S.Creatinine, bilirubin tests, SGOT, TSH, urine examination, Chest X-ray, and diet counseling.
Executive Health Checkup Package: ₹4000. Builds on the Master package, adding HbA1C, S.Urea, S.Uric acid, Liver Function Test (LFT), and an ultrasound of the abdomen. It also includes a PSA for men.
Premium Health Checkup Package: ₹4500. Expands on the Executive package by adding GFR, Protein Creatine Ratio, Vitamin D, ECHO Cardiogram, a Pulmonary Function Test, and cardiologist/orthopedic consultations if needed. It also offers a 50% discount on TMT.
Well Women Health Checkup Package: ₹5500. This is a specialized package for women. It includes a complete haemogram, fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, serum calcium, LFT, serum uric acid, Vitamin D, mammography, sonomammography, ultrasound of the abdomen & pelvis, Chest X-ray, Pap smear, T3/T4/TSH, ECG, and a Pulmonary Function Test.
